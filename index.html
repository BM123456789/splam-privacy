Splam! Support

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Questions about Splam!? Email floppydiskspy@gmail.com.

1. No Personal Data Collected

Splam! does not require an account or login, and does not collect, sell, or transmit your name, email address, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. This version does not include third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

2. Local On-Device Storage

Your high scores, Daily Challenge best score, Golden Flies balance, unlocked critters, and control/sound preferences are stored solely on your device using local storage. Deleting the app removes this local data.

3. Sharing Run Clips & Splam Cards

When you tap Share Run Clip or Splam Card, gameplay video or snapshot frames are rendered locally on your device, temporarily saved to your device cache, and passed to your system Share Sheet. We never upload, view, or store your clips or images. If you choose “Save Video” or “Save Image,” iOS saves the file to your Photos library only with your permission.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because we do not collect personal information from any player, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region).

5. Future Updates

If optional advertising, analytics, or online leaderboards are added in a future release, this Privacy Policy and any required consent prompts (such as Apple App Tracking Transparency) will be updated before those features go live.

6. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at floppydiskspy@gmail.com.
